# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Clickable mock only: static HTML/CSS/JS, published as a private claude.ai Artifact. The real app's stack is undecided.

## Users

Primary: a founder of a small team, between tasks, with about five minutes. They want something good to post right now, then get back to building. They run several live products.

Others on the team (marketing, support) can pick up and post too, and everyone sees who posted what.

## Product Purpose

Post maker turns a product's live app link into a few ready, high-quality social posts each day: copy for each platform plus designed visuals (carousels, short clips, close-up images) made from the product's real screens. The team copies and posts by hand.

Success: someone posts what it made with at most a small edit. If they have to rewrite it, it failed.

## Positioning

Competitors either turn git commits into text posts (for developers) or turn a public marketing page into generic posts. Post maker wins on output quality for a team: real close-ups of the product, visuals in the product's own brand, copy in its voice, and shared memory so posts do not repeat. A local-repo input exists but is secondary, for developers.

## Operating Context

- Each visit: open it, see today's best few posts (3 to 5) for one product, best first, copy, post, mark posted, leave.
- Posts are previewed the way they look in the X, LinkedIn and Instagram feeds, including the "see more" cutoff.
- A product is added once by pasting its link (optionally a demo login); it learns what the product does, its voice, colours and screens.
- Team flow: draft, approved by someone, posted by someone on a platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Never auto-posts.** No platform APIs, no scheduling. Auto-posting got the owner's account banned once. Not negotiable.
- Formats: carousel (LinkedIn, Instagram), short clip with burned-in captions, single close-up image; sizes 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16.
- A quality check drops weak posts (generic, banned words, unverifiable claims) before anyone sees them, and flags posts that use example details.
- Undecided: where it runs, image templates vs generated per post, pricing, whether other teams will pay.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Post maker (working name, kept by the owner on 2026-11-18).
- Feel reference chosen by the owner: Arc / Raycast (playful, bold colour, delightful details).

## Evidence on Hand

- No customers, testimonials or usage numbers exist. Do not invent them. Team member names in the mock (Ada, Tunde) are examples.

## Product Principles

1. Quality over volume: a few posts worth posting beat many worth skipping.
2. Show the real product, never a generic stock look.
3. The person posts. The tool prepares and steps aside.
4. Respect the five-minute visit: the best post is the first thing on screen.
5. Honest by default: flag anything someone could not check.
