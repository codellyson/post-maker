# Post maker mock (app screens)

Scope: the clickable mock at mock/index.html: today, products, add product, what it knows, posted. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a founder between tasks with five minutes; open, take today's best post, copy, post by hand, mark posted, leave.
Constraints: never auto-posts; example data only (Passalong is the one filled-in product); the posts themselves are rendered in each product's own brand, not the app's.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today's best few posts as live tiles under a giant product name; the category default (a white list-plus-editor like Typefully) is refused.
OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground, white ink, flat cyan, magenta, lime, orange and purple fields with no gradients, bevels or borders; giant light lowercase type cropped at the right edge; cyan owns every live and selected state; square line-icon buttons; dot-matrix texture only inside tiles.
STORY: The founder sees which product, how many posts are ready, and the best one already open with its feed preview; they copy it, post it, mark it posted.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (wordmark, nav with square icons, my products with counts). Main: giant lowercase product name as a pivot header, the next product peeking at the right edge; under it a 2x2 grid of post tiles (best tile double width); right column: the selected post's title, platform pivot, copy text as the primary cyan block button, then the realistic feed preview whose text edits in place.
FORM: Metro typographic tiles (challenger, chosen by the user); signature moves: pivot header panorama between products, and live tiles that flip between the post's visual and its first line. seed:ada8adbc69498075
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
